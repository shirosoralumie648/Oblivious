## Unsupported / Deferred Surfaces

- Trigger listener replay/idempotency, retention policy APIs, and full debugger consolidation remain separate release-gate work.

## Known Residual Risk

The proof uses a temporary local Postgres container, not a deployed target environment with production credentials, migrations, and traffic. Keep target-environment replay verification in the commercial release gate.
