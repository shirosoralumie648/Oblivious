## Claim Rules

- A milestone may claim completion only for requirements whose current repository evidence, automated verification, and required runtime smoke are recorded.
- v04 Commercial Foundation may claim tenant/security/migration/CI foundation completion after `CI-01` and `DOC-03` pass.
- v04 must not claim final commercial readiness or commercial completeness.
- v07 is complete and v08 Product Completeness is complete after Phase 30 strict verification.
- v05 may claim Relay Authority Gate completion only after route classification, production fail-closed, provider-bypass checks, endpoint auth/rate-limit/audit, settlement/refund behavior, and v05 Relay Authority Gate closeout evidence all pass.
- v06 may claim Billing And Monetization Gate completion only because subscription lifecycle, top-up fulfillment, refunds, invoices, failed-payment states, plan changes, Marketplace settlement, payout state, moderation workflows, admin billing inspection, and v06 closeout evidence have all passed Phase 17-20 verification.
- v07 may claim Operations Gate completion only after orchestration runtime proof, backup/restore smoke, observability, alerts/dashboards/SLOs, release/rollback runbooks, incident/DR runbooks, and v07 closeout evidence all pass.
- v08 may claim `PROD-01` only after default built-in Agent tools are proven real or disabled by tests, docs, and quality gates; `web_search` and `http_request` cannot be counted as commercial-ready while disabled by default.
- v08 may claim `PROD-02` only after durable Agent workflow schema, service state transitions, status APIs, approval/reject/retry behavior, memory evidence, failure evidence, and cross-tenant denial tests pass with DB-backed evidence.
- v08 may claim `PROD-03` only after Knowledge ingestion stores Relay-produced embeddings, retrieval uses pgvector vector search under organization scope, API/UI results expose source citations, and `.planning/phases/27-knowledge-product-promise-alignment/27-VERIFICATION.md` records backend, frontend, docs, and diff evidence.
- v08 may claim `PROD-04` only after active Chat, Agent/SOLO, Knowledge, Admin, and Marketplace journeys have focused frontend tests for commercial loading/error/empty/action states, quota or budget context, review and settlement boundaries, docs gates, and `.planning/phases/28-commercial-ux-and-journey-hardening/28-VERIFICATION.md`.
- v08 may claim `PROD-05` only after README, public docs, onboarding, pricing, operator guides, API docs, architecture contracts, commercial gates, quality gates, stale-doc scans, and `.planning/phases/29-public-docs-onboarding-pricing-and-operator-guides/29-VERIFICATION.md` prove alignment with implemented tenant, Relay, billing, Marketplace, operations, and product behavior.
- v08 may claim `PROD-06`, `AUDIT-01`, Product Completeness Gate, and final commercial readiness only after `scripts/verify-commercial-completion.sh` passes with deploy validation and backup/restore smoke enabled, no `COMMERCIAL_COMPLETION_ALLOW_ENV_SKIPS=true`, and `.planning/phases/30-end-to-end-commercial-journey-and-final-audit/30-VERIFICATION.md` records exact command evidence.
- Any future readiness claim must link to exact files, commands, environment class, migration status, pass/fail result, skipped checks, and residual risk.
