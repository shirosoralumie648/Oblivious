## 2024-05-18 - Extracting Intl formatters outside component render cycle\n**Learning:** Instantiating `Intl.NumberFormat` and `Intl.DateTimeFormat` inside React render loops or frequently called format functions creates unnecessary garbage collection overhead and CPU spikes. The codebase was re-creating these expensive instances on every list render (e.g. for every package or invoice row).\n**Action:** Always declare `Intl` formatter instances as module-level constants to ensure they are created exactly once and safely reused across all component instances.
