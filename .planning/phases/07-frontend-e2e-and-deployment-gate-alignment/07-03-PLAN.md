## Threat Model

| Threat | Severity | Mitigation in this plan |
|--------|----------|-------------------------|
| CI appears green while skipping the real web/E2E/server commands | High | Pin CI steps to `bash scripts/check.sh web`, `bash scripts/test.sh web`, `pnpm --dir src/web test:e2e`, `bash scripts/check.sh server`, and `bash scripts/test.sh server`. |
| Docker builds fail in restricted-network environments after v03.2 proof | High | Preserve `OBLIVIOUS_IMAGE_REGISTRY_PREFIX`, `OBLIVIOUS_GOPROXY`, and `OBLIVIOUS_GOSUMDB` build args and validate with the exact override command. |
| Compose smoke validates only configuration, not a live server | High | Keep `scripts/deploy-validate.sh` build/up/smoke flow and `/healthz` probe against `BASE_URL`. |
| Secret defaults leak into committed config | Medium | Keep placeholders only in `config/.env.example` and compose sample values; do not commit real `.env` files or credentials. |
</threat_model>
