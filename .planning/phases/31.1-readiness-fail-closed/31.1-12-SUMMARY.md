## Decisions Made

- A semantic-cache hit is a billable AI success path and must consume current server-owned model authority before cache I/O or success accounting.
- Context cache metadata cannot select a model identity different from the authorized route model; mismatch returns stable `capability_unknown` before lookup.
- Model/catalog authority is re-resolved after potentially long billing work; Provider transport authorization remains a separate immediate guard and neither verdict is generation-pinned.
