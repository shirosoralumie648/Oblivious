## Accomplishments

- Added `NewReadinessHandlers` and concrete Admin/app DTOs. Strict production composition serves manager-backed `/livez` and `/readyz`, authenticated `/api/v1/admin/readiness`, and authenticated `/api/v1/app/readiness/capabilities`; excluded capabilities are omitted from the app projection and stable readiness codes never expose probe details.

The inventory includes all twelve standalone roots as excluded entrypoint rows and distinct builtin/custom-HTTP/local-Python/Python-sandbox/MCP executor seams.
- Added `inspect-readiness`, `report-readiness`, `verify-readiness-snapshot`, and `report-deployment` commands. Inputs are bounded strict JSON, identity/profile are resolver-owned, runtime authorization is never seeded from snapshots/reports, readiness/deployment details remain separate registered schemas, and writes use `AtomicWriter`.
- Registered deployment details in the shared report registry with exact monolith workload/probe/migration/harness invariants.
