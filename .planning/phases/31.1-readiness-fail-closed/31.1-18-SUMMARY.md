## Decisions Made

- Keep configuration selection declarative and exact: optional cohorts are all-or-none, while web-search provider rows are exactly-one.
- Validate descriptor owner, boundary, EffectID, capability, disposition, and structural metadata before accepting registration or coverage.
- Follow only explicit local call paths declared in manifest metadata; do not infer call relationships from comments, strings, owner names, or arbitrary call-graph guesses.
- Enumerate registrations from source data flow before consulting the specification map; known rows then require exact structure, while unknown rows remain visible to the extra-static gate.
- Qualify method paths by concrete receiver and accept unqualified names only for free functions.
- Preserve the Plan 19 boundary: no producer or real `BuildRuntime` composition file was changed to satisfy this contract.
