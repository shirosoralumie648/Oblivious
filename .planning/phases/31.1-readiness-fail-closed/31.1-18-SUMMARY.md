## Deviations from Plan

**Revision 1 review deviation:** post-completion review found that function-name-only indexing allowed receiver collisions, constructor-wide validation allowed sibling capability evidence borrowing, and specification-driven iteration hid unknown source registrations. The fix remained inside the three Plan 18-owned files, added source-first enumeration and exact receiver/binding proof, and did not cross the Plan 19 producer/composition boundary.

**Revision 2 review deviation:** follow-up review found that known-ID validation discarded the exact enumerated registration and rescanned arbitrary constructor literals, while generated rows still used a generic Resolve heuristic. Revision 2 removed both rescan paths and bound validation to the registration and lexical Resolve data flow without changing producer code.

**Revision 3 review deviation:** final review found that omitted optional cohorts could pass without an explicit disablement, call-name proof could borrow dead or wrong-object calls, descriptor provenance could occur after consumption, and map/input order could change the reported first failure. Revision 3 closed all four gaps within the Plan 18 contract, manifest, and tests without changing producer behavior.

**Revision 4 review deviation:** follow-up review found that resolver/registrar receiver identity and helper reachability were still underconstrained, and that stale earlier scalar/collection definitions could be reported after a later write. Revision 4 bound those paths to AST objects and used the last reaching state before consumption.

**Revision 5 review deviation:** final provenance review found name-based scalar and collection lookup could cross lexical scopes, while unknown-condition writes could be accepted from a single path. Revision 5 moved reaching definitions to exact `ast.Object` identity and merges conditional path states fail closed.

**Revision 6 review deviation:** loop review found Range membership assumed one iteration and `For` ignored its Post statement, allowing zero-iteration and loop-carried state to certify descriptors. Revision 6 merges zero/multiple-iteration paths conservatively and preserves generated production rows only for statically known Range cardinality.
