## Decisions Made

- Generated symbols are passed by production callers; shared wrappers cannot infer operation identity from runtime paths or generic type parameters.
- Decoder Accept headers are derived from the declared response contract and contradictory caller headers fail before `fetch`.
- Non-OK handling remains one JSON-envelope parser; malformed or non-JSON bodies do not enter public messages or details.
- Existing default JSON behavior remains for source compatibility until subsequent plans migrate the remaining production owners.
