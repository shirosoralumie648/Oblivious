## Decisions Made

- Production code calls only `register*RouteSurfaces`; legacy `register*Routes(*ServeMux, ...)` symbols construct a local test registrar and delegate without metadata, direct mount, global lookup, or production ownership.
- Routing fallbacks are tracked separately from descriptor mounts. They apply the owning route's auth policy but intentionally do not apply business guards or claim capability/descriptor identity.
- `HEAD` remains Go `ServeMux`'s implicit behavior for GET registrations. Explicit HEAD wildcard fallbacks are omitted because they can conflict with static GET patterns.
- The Gateway operation retains distinct JSON and SSE success identities at status 200. Duplicate rejection is keyed by status plus normalized media/schema identity rather than status alone.
- Group A mutation tests derive their baseline from the production registrar snapshot and compare-only manifest. Test-only reconstruction is used only for injected fault evidence and cannot install production routes.
