## Decisions Made

- Contract identity comes only from the generated operation symbol resolved by the compiler checker. A URL, local object, or TypeScript generic cannot authorize classification.
- Method/path checks validate the invocation against the selected generated contract; they never perform runtime metadata lookup or replace the generated symbol.
- Shared transport implementation files are explicit owner dispositions rather than counted as product call sites. The composition-only provider remains a tested non-caller.
- Pair validation compares sidecar, source, and config digests while keeping transport taxonomy and exposure projection digests independent.
