The sidecar schema is exactly `frontend-surface-sidecar/v1` with `sourceScope`, `extractor`, `operations`, `exposures`, `generatedConsumers`, and `unresolved`. `verify-frontend-surface-sidecar.sh --self-check` is the sole Node test wrapper. It runs `node --test --test-reporter=tap scripts/frontend_surface_sidecar.test.mjs`, parses TAP plan/test/case totals, consumes structured taxonomy/mutation counters emitted by the test, and fails unless the TAP test/case total and every required transport taxonomy and negative mutation count are greater than zero.

Production Stage A has an exact expected-owner set of 25 files: the five Plan 04 production owners, nine Plan 11 owners, eight Plan 12 owners, Plan 10 `features/releaseProjection/releaseProjection.tsx`, and the two public marketing auth owners from Plan 15. Every entry is either a resolved exact-symbol transport caller/owner or an explicit compiler-proven non-caller disposition with reason and test. Set equality is bidirectional: a missing expected owner or a newly discovered production caller outside the set fails.

Before report work, require Phase 31 `RegisterDetails`, trusted resolvers, `SurfaceReportV1`, and `AtomicWriter` as declared. The report constructors are side-effect-free and accept no output path or caller release identity.
