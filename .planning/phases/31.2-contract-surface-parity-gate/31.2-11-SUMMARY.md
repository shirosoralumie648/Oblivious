## Decisions Made

- Generated operation symbols are imported directly by each caller and passed through typed transport objects. Helper functions only choose codecs from the supplied operation; they do not reconstruct method, path, schema, media, or status identity.
- `providers.tsx` remains unchanged because it is composition-only. The final preflight uses the verifier's explicit non-caller disposition instead of the plan's incorrect `--expect-owner` invocation.
- Agent tool capability identity remains server-owned. Response normalization requires a non-empty `capabilityId`; create/update serialization selects business fields and never spreads the response object.
- `continue-plan` sends no placeholder `{}` body because its generated operation declares no request media/schema.
- Durable Knowledge ingestion runtime behavior takes precedence over the stale historical OpenAPI row; the canonical source and every generated/verifier consumer now agree on `202` job metadata.
