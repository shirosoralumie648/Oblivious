## 2024-10-03 - Added tooltips to icon-only buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons often lack clear context for users, particularly when relying on screen readers or when icons are ambiguous. Adding tooltips to these buttons significantly improves UX by providing immediate, visually accessible text describing the action.
**Action:** In the future, always accompany icon-only buttons with standard tooltips (`TooltipProvider`, `Tooltip`, `TooltipTrigger`, `TooltipContent`) to ensure the action is clear to all users.
